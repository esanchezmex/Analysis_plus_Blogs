# Football Analytics & Blog Posts

A collection of data-driven football analyses, combining R and Python with strong opinions.

---

## Analyses

### [Is Emiliano Martínez Actually Good at Saving Penalties?](./2024_07_16_Penalty_Analysis/)
*July 2024* — A data-driven hate piece. Using K-means clustering on 273 international penalties, I define what makes a "good" penalty and investigate whether Emi Martínez is truly elite at saving them—or just elite at making players choke.

**Tools:** R, Python, StatsBomb data, K-means clustering

---

### [Can Aston Villa Pip Spurs to the Top 4?](./2024_03_10_Villa's_Top4_Argument/)
*March 2024* — A nervous Spurs fan's deep dive into Villa's top-4 credentials before a crucial head-to-head. Examines Watkins vs Haaland, Villa's overperforming attackers, and whether the underlying numbers favor Tottenham's late-season push.

**Tools:** R, FBref data via worldfootballR

---

### [Reflecting on Klopp's Final Season](./2024_01_29_Klopp_Leaving/)
*January 2024* — A statistical farewell to Jurgen Klopp. Breaking down Liverpool's goal threat (Salah, Jota, Núñez), their creative engine (Trent), and how "Big Dom" Szoboszlai fit in perfectly—written by a Spurs fan who respects greatness.

**Tools:** R, FBref data via worldfootballR

---

## About

All analyses use publicly available data from [StatsBomb](https://github.com/statsbomb/open-data) and [FBref](https://fbref.com/). Written with a mix of statistical rigor and unapologetic football opinions.
